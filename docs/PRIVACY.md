# AI Leak Guard: privacy policy

*Last updated: 22 October 2026*

AI Leak Guard is a browser extension that warns you before you paste or send confidential information to AI chat tools.

## What the extension reads

On the supported AI chat sites (listed in the extension's description), AI Leak Guard looks at the text you paste or are about to send, so it can check it for things like passwords, bank details or personal data. This check happens entirely inside your browser, on your device.

## What it stores

- **Your settings:** language, which checks are switched on, and your list of protected terms. They are kept in your browser's extension storage. If you have browser sync switched on, your browser may sync these settings between your own devices, as it does for other extension settings.
- **Counters:** how many warnings were shown, and how often text was masked or stopped. Only the numbers are stored, locally, never the text itself.
- **Organisation settings:** if your employer manages the extension, its settings come from your organisation's browser policy.

## What it does not do

- It does not send the text you paste or type anywhere.
- It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
- It has no analytics, tracking or advertising, and it makes no network requests of its own.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension removes its settings and counters from your browser.
